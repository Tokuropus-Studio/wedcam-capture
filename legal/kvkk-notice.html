KVKK Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Prepared under Article 10 of Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK"), for people in Türkiye.

Data controller: WedCam, [postal address to be completed], tokuropus@gmail.com.

Personal data processed

Identity and contact data (email), customer transaction data (plan, credits, choices), visual records (photographs, garment photographs, Outputs), transaction security data (IP, device and system logs), and consent records. Face photographs, and descriptions of the face, body, marks and tattoos derived from them, may count as biometric data or special categories of personal data, so we apply the safeguards for special categories to them.

Purposes

Creating and performing the membership contract; producing the images you request; running payments and credits; information security; meeting legal duties; answering requests.

Legal grounds and collection method

Data is collected electronically through the app and the partner page. Grounds: establishing and performing a contract (Art. 5/2-c), legal obligation (Art. 5/2-ç), establishing and protecting a right (Art. 5/2-e), legitimate interest (Art. 5/2-f). For face data: explicit consent (Art. 6/2). See the Explicit Consent Statement.

Transfers abroad

Data is hosted and processed abroad, mainly in the United States. Recipients: hosting (Supabase), AI model providers, subscription infrastructure (RevenueCat), payment and distribution (Apple), cloud hosting and GitHub. Transfers take place under Article 9 KVKK with appropriate safeguards, using the standard contracts announced by the Board and making the required notifications. Explicit consent is also obtained for special categories of data.

Retention

Photographs, descriptions and Outputs are kept for at most 1 year; account data until the account is deleted. At the end of the period data is deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

Your rights (Article 11)

To learn whether your data is processed; to request information; to learn the purpose and whether data is used accordingly; to know the third parties who receive it; to ask for correction if incomplete or wrong, and for deletion or destruction; to ask that recipients be told of this; to object to a result against you arising from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage from unlawful processing.

How to apply

Send requests to tokuropus@gmail.com or the postal address above, in line with the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller. They are concluded free of charge within 30 days. If we do not answer or you find the answer insufficient, you may apply to the Personal Data Protection Board.
