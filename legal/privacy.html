Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Policy explains how WedCam ("WedCam", "we") handles personal data in the WedCam app and partner camera page. Contact: tokuropus@gmail.com, [postal address to be completed]. Our separate Face Data Policy forms part of this Policy. Users in Türkiye should also read the KVKK notice.

The short version

  • We use your photos to make the images you ask for, and nothing else.
  • Face photos are sent to AI service providers only after you give explicit consent.
  • Everything is deleted after one year, or sooner if you ask.
  • We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Partners and other people in photos

If someone's photo is added to your account, this Policy applies to them too. The account holder must have their permission. A partner invited by link gives consent on the link page and may contact us to exercise any right below.

Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

  • Contract: account, generation, plans.
  • Explicit consent (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a), where face data is special category data): face photos and derived descriptions. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: security, fraud prevention and legal claims.
  • Legal obligation: tax and regulatory duties.

International transfers

Your data is processed in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires, we rely on approved safeguards such as European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK addendum, and for Türkiye the standard contract under Article 9 of Law No. 6698, or on your explicit consent.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may: access your data, get a copy, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to a regulator.

Inside the app:

  • Withdraw face photo consent: My Account. Your uploaded face photos are deleted and no new images can be made.
  • Delete your account: My Account. Your account, photos, garment photos, descriptions, Outputs and credit history are removed.

Everything else: write to tokuropus@gmail.com. We reply within one month, or sooner if your local law requires. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a right, and we may need to confirm your identity.

Deleted data

Deletion takes effect promptly in live systems. Residual copies in encrypted backups are overwritten within 30 days. We may keep data longer only where the law requires it or to establish or defend legal claims, and then only as needed.

Security

Files are private and reached only through short-lived signed links. Each account can reach only its own data. Traffic is encrypted. Partner links store only a hash of their secret. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify you and regulators of a breach as the law requires.

Children

The Service is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly process data of anyone under 18. Write to tokuropus@gmail.com if you believe we have, and we will delete it.

Changes

We will announce material changes in the app before they take effect.

Complaints

Write to us first. You may also complain to your data protection authority, such as your EU supervisory authority, the UK ICO, or the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority.
