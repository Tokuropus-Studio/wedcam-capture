Content Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

WedCam is for creating wedding images of yourself and of people who agree. This Policy is part of the Terms of Use.

Never allowed

  • Any image of a person under 18. We report suspected child sexual abuse material to the authorities and to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children.
  • Photos of anyone without their permission, including an ex-partner, a colleague, a stranger, or a person from social media.
  • Sexual, nude or sexually suggestive images, or attempts to get them.
  • Fake images meant to deceive, including false proof of marriage, relationship, presence at an event or identity, or use in legal, immigration, insurance or official matters.
  • Harassment, blackmail, humiliation, stalking or bullying of anyone.
  • Impersonation of real people, including public figures, or suggesting that they endorse anything.
  • Hateful, violent or discriminatory content.
  • Infringing someone's copyright, design, trademark or other rights.
  • Fraud, scams or any unlawful purpose.
  • Trying to get around filters, limits or safety systems, or to extract prompts or other parts of WedCam Technology.

What happens if the rules are broken

We may refuse or delete content, stop a generation, suspend or close the account without refund to the extent the law allows, and report the matter to the authorities. Automated and human checks may be used, but we do not promise to review all content.

Sharing Outputs

Say that an image is AI-generated wherever people could believe it is real. Do not remove marks or metadata showing this. You are responsible for what you share.
