Face Data Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Policy is our public retention schedule and destruction guideline for face data, and explains the informed consent we ask for. It applies to account holders and to anyone whose photo is submitted. It is written to meet the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act and similar laws in Texas, Washington and elsewhere. Those laws may or may not treat our processing as biometric; we apply these safeguards to all face data either way.

What we collect

"Face Data" means:

  • the five reference photographs of each person, four of them close views of the face;
  • the features of a face and body in those photographs as read by AI systems, which may include measurements or scans of face geometry made by those systems;
  • a written description derived from the photographs, covering face structure, skin marks, tattoos, piercings, hair, beard and build.

Why we collect it

Only to generate the images you ask for and keep the people in them recognisable. We do not use Face Data to identify people, to verify identity, to compare a face with others, to build a searchable database, for advertising, or to train AI models.

Who receives it

Face Data is sent to AI model providers acting for us, and stored with our hosting provider, as listed in the Privacy Policy. They may not use it for their own purposes. We never sell, lease, trade or profit from Face Data. We do not disclose it to anyone else unless you consent, the law or a valid court order requires it, or it is needed to complete a transaction you asked for.

Consent

Before any Face Data is collected, the person gives written consent by ticking an unticked box and tapping to continue. The box states that the photos will be sent to an AI image generation provider and kept for one year. The electronic record of that act is their written release. A partner using a link gives this consent personally on the page. We record the time and the wording version.

The account holder also confirms that everyone shown has given permission and is over 18.

Retention and destruction schedule

DataDestroyed
Reference photographsAutomatically 1 year after upload or latest retake, or at once if consent is withdrawn or the account is deleted
Derived descriptionAutomatically 1 year after creation, or at once if consent is withdrawn or the account is deleted
AI provider copiesUnder provider terms, generally within 30 days
Backup copiesOverwritten within 30 days of deletion

In every case Face Data is permanently destroyed when the purpose of collection has been met, and no later than one year after the person's last interaction with us unless the law requires otherwise. An automated job runs daily to do this.

Withdrawing consent

Use My Account → Withdraw my face photo consent, or write to tokuropus@gmail.com. Uploaded face photographs and derived descriptions are deleted, and no new images can be made. Images already generated remain in your gallery until they expire or you delete your account; write to us if you want them removed sooner. A partner who has no account may write to us and we will delete their Face Data.

Security

We protect Face Data with at least the same care as our other confidential information, including private storage, encryption in transit and strict access controls, and at a standard no lower than is reasonable in our industry.
